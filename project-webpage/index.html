01

Robust estimation in urban canyons

GNSS multipath, unreliable visual associations, and broken carrier-phase continuity can introduce errors that persist in tightly coupled estimators. SeA-RVINS addresses these sources of error in a fixed-lag factor graph that combines raw RTK GNSS, inertial measurements, and stereo vision.

A semantic-aware learned frontend screens tracks before they enter the graph. Three configurable robust formulations retain the shared-pivot correlation of double-differenced GNSS measurements. A hybrid ambiguity-continuation strategy shares ambiguity states over short, verified arcs and softly links successive arcs.

Reliable visual tracks

Semantic screening and geometric verification reject unreliable associations before they become persistent landmark factors.

Correlation-preserving robustness

Batch, scalar, and latent-pivot formulations apply Dynamic Covariance Scaling to double-differenced code and phase observations.

Verified ambiguity continuity

Bounded, continuity-verified arcs and random-walk links retain temporal information; broken or unverifiable continuity starts independent states.

02

System architecture

GNSS, IMU, and stereo observations enter a 6-second fixed-lag factor graph. Integer ambiguity hypotheses are validated before accepted fixes are applied.

Semantic-aware learned stereo frontend

Semantic masking, learned feature matching, and geometric checks screen stereo and temporal tracks before landmark admission.
